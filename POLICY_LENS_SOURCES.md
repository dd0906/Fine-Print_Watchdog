# India Policy Lens — source and scope notes

Catalogue snapshot: **2026-10-19**

The initial catalogue contains **54 curated references** across central contract/commercial law, consumer and e-commerce, digital/data/IP, finance/lending, employment, property, tax and sector-specific topics. It is designed as an extensible starting catalogue, not a complete collection of every Indian law or government policy.

## How matches work

The backend searches extracted document text for literal keyword triggers recorded for each entry. It does not call the language model, retrieve law text at runtime, validate a clause, or determine whether a law applies. A match is a research lead only.

## Important current-source notes

- The Reserve Bank of India [Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848&Mode=0) identifies itself as the current Directions and states that it consolidates earlier instructions; the 2022 Guidelines page itself says that earlier circular has been repealed. The catalogue points to the 2025 Directions, not the superseded 2022 circular.
- The official [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025) specify staggered commencement. The product therefore warns users to check whether the relevant provisions are in force on the relevant date.
- The Ministry of Labour and Employment’s [Year End Review 2025](https://labour.gov.in/sites/default/files/pib2209767.pdf) states the four Labour Codes took effect on 21 November 2025. State rules and transaction/worker coverage still require checking.
- Some entries link to a title-specific search page on India Code or a regulator’s consolidated listings rather than a single static PDF. Users should open the official result and check amendments, rules, commencement notifications and exceptions.

## Known boundaries

- This is a curated **central-law and selected regulator-guidance** catalogue; it is not exhaustive.
- State/UT tenancy laws, stamp-duty schedules, registration rules, Shops and Establishments laws, local licensing, state amendments and sector notifications are not individually indexed.
- Some areas change rapidly. Current source version, commencement, amendment history, regulated-entity status and the date of the transaction must be checked before relying on a reference.
- This feature is not legal advice and does not establish legality, enforceability, compliance or non-compliance. A lawyer or appropriate professional should review material decisions.
